Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade from Community to Professional?

Yes, you can upgrade at any time by simply applying a license key. All your data and configurations will be preserved.

What happens after the 30-day active period?

The Community Edition switches to Read-only mode. Scans are paused, but all existing results remain visible. You can extend the period once manually.

Is the Professional Version on-premise?

Currently, YADS is primarily designed as a self-hosted solution (Docker/Kubernetes) to ensure you maintain full control over your scan data.
