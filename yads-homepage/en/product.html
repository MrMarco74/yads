Complete Coverage

All Scan Modules in Detail

🔎 Reconnaissance

  • Domain Discovery: Finds subdomains via Passive DNS & Brute Force.
  • Cloud Assets: Detects AWS S3 Buckets, Azure Blobs & GCP Resources.
  • Crawler: Deep analysis of website structures and links.
  • Wayback Machine: Searches historical data for forgotten endpoints.
  • Certificate Search: Uses crt.sh for Transparency Logs.

🛡️ Vulnerabilities

  • Nuclei Engine: 5000+ Templates for CVEs, Misconfigurations & Exposures.
  • Port Scanner: Stealth Nmap Scans for open services.
  • SSL/TLS Analysis: Checks for weak ciphers and expiring certificates.
  • CVE Lookup: Matches installed software against CVE databases.

🧠 Intelligence

  • Tech Stack: Fingerprinting of CMS, Servers, and Frameworks.
  • Typosquatting: Monitoring for phishing domains (e.g., g0ogle.com).
  • Visual OSINT: Reverse image search for brand logos.
  • DNS Monitor: Monitoring changes in A, AAAA, MX, TXT records.

📋 Compliance & Extras

  • Compliance Check: SOC2 & ISO 27001 Readiness Indicators.
  • Dead Links: Detection of Broken Link Hijacking.
  • Brand Monitor: Identifies brand mentions on the web.
  • Email Security: SPF, DMARC & Spoofing Protection Analysis.
