What is PQC?

Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) refers to cryptographic algorithms resistant to attacks from quantum computers. NIST finalized PQC standards in 2024 (ML-KEM / CRYSTALS-Kyber for key exchange, ML-DSA for digital signatures). Hybrid schemes combine classical (X25519, P-256) with PQC algorithms for a transition period.
